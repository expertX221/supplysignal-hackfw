# SupplySignal — 90-second demo script

0–10 seconds: “A supplier changes a delivery date. Which production job is now at risk? SupplySignal turns that small signal into a clear next action.” Show the dashboard.

10–25 seconds: “The exception desk ranks late and short order lines. Supplier health summarizes the current confirmations, while the delivery timeline shows what is arriving next.” Show the health view and timeline.

25–45 seconds: “PO-1051 is critical because its control boards arrive four days after the job needs them. The review explains the dates and source order line instead of hiding the decision behind a score.” Open the priority order.

45–60 seconds: “The operator can edit a supplier follow-up draft, record an action, and keep planning notes. Nothing is sent automatically.” Show the editable draft and planner notes.

60–75 seconds: “When the supplier confirms an earlier delivery, save the new date. SupplySignal recalculates the risk and updates the desk, timeline, and supplier view.” Change PO-1051's ETA to November 4, 2026, then save.

75–90 seconds: “Import CSV orders or export the action plan. This prototype uses transparent rules and browser-local data. Next, we will add source-linked AI extraction from supplier emails and an ERP adapter.” Show the desk and project links.
